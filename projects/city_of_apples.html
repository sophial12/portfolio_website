City of Apples

20”x16”, Oil pastel

A still life study that captures the interplay of light and shadow on organic forms. Using oil pastels, I explored geometric shapes and tonal layering to create depth and texture, highlighting the vibrant colors and natural beauty of the apples against the stark contrast of the glass cups.

Role
Artist
Year
2023
Tools
Oil pastel

Project Details

The brief, approach, and results

Brief

In this still life drawing, I explored the balance between intense light and shadow, and organic forms against hard surfaces. I also investigated the tension created with the apples and the delicate stacking of glass cups.

Outcome

Received a gold key from the Scholastic Art & Writing Awards in the Oil Pastel category.
