JustAir Campaign

A marketing campaign for JustAir's new asthma and air quality program

JustAir is a company focused on educating urban residents about air pollution sources and promoting healthier lifestyle choices. They are starting a new asthma and air quality program, and are looking for ways to raise awareness about the program and increase recruitment of participants from Wayne County.

Project Details

The brief, approach, and results

Brief

JustAir, a company started by someone who grew up in Detroit and experienced asthma firsthand, is launching a new asthma and air quality program. The program aims to collect data on certain areas in Wayne County where asthma attacks are most frequent. The goal is to raise awareness about the program and increase recruitment of participants from Wayne County, with the target audience being individuals ages 16-26 in with asthma.

Approach

I started with demographic research of asthma patients in Wayne County, as well as looking into air pollution sources and health impacts. I then brainstormed ideas for the campaign messaging and visual style, focusing on creating a friendly and approachable tone to resonate with the target audience. I created a series of flyers and social media assets to promote the program, incorporating illustrations and infographics to convey key information.

After a meeting with JustAir to gather feedback, I realized that my flyers were too text-heavy and cluttered, despite having only the necessary information. To address this, I decided to create business cards that provided a concise overview of the program and directed interested individuals to JustAir's air quality program pages for more information.

Outcome

The campaign effectively raises awareness about the program and encourages participation from the target audience in Wayne County. I received positive feedback from JustAir on the design and messaging of the campaign materials, and my social media assets helped increase engagement and reach among the target audience.

Deliverables

  • Posters and flyers
  • Social media assets
  • Business cards
