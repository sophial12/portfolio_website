LeDog LeSoup Case Study

A detailed case study deliverable on a local Ann Arbor restaurant

Prompt: LeDog LeSoup has been a staple of Ann Arbor since 1979. However, due to their location, the density of lunch/dinner places on U-M’s campus, and their very limited/specific hours, they are having a difficult time increasing their number of student customers. Design a marketing deliverable to help LeDog LeSoup advertise to college students in the Ann Arbor area.

Project Details

The brief, approach, and results

Brief

LeDog LeSoup is a beloved local restaurant in Ann Arbor, known for its hearty soups and sandwiches. However, its location and limited hours have made it challenging to attract a younger, student demographic. The objective was to create a marketing deliverable that would resonate with college students, highlighting the restaurant's unique offerings and encouraging them to visit.

Approach

I started my process by researching LeDog LeSoup's current branding and marketing strategies, as well as analyzing competitors targeting the student demographic. I noticed a few weaker areas in their current approach regarding their lack of social media presence and modern design elements:

  • The current logo includes a hotdog but not soup, even though they are most well known for their soups.
  • The website is outdated and difficult to navigate.
  • Instagram does not have a lot of reach.
I then brainstormed ideas for a fresh, modern logo that incorporated both soup and a hotdog, aiming to create a more balanced representation of the restaurant's offerings. I experimented with various color schemes and typography styles to appeal to a younger audience, focusing on bold, vibrant colors and clean, sans-serif fonts. After finalizing the logo, I designed a series of marketing materials, including posters and social media assets, that highlighted the restaurant's unique selling points, such as their homemade soups and cozy atmosphere. I developed a simple, user-friendly website layout to improve the online presence and make it easier for students to find information about the menu, hours, and location. I also created a brand toolkit to ensure consistency across all marketing channels.

Outcome

There are no quantitative metrics becauase this was a case studey project, but the qualitative feedback from peers and instructors was positive. This case study was part of my application and helped me get accepted into my marketing club, Michigan Advertising and Marketing (MAM).

Deliverables

  • Flyers and social media assets
  • Modernized logo
  • Redesigned website
