Down the Drain

18” x 24” x 4.5”, Mixed media

This piece was inspired by my lifelong struggle with eczema, a skin condition that has caused me discomfort and frustration for years. The artwork captures the sensation of relief I experience when I run my hands under cold water to soothe the itching and inflammation.

By using mixed media, I aimed to convey the tactile nature of eczema and the emotional journey of living with a chronic condition. The piece invites viewers to empathize with the physical and emotional challenges of eczema while also celebrating moments of relief and self-care.

Project Details

The brief, approach, and results

Brief

Eczema is a skin condition that I began suffering from seven years ago. It has traveled all around my body, but has affected my hands for the longest amount of time. The itchy, inflamed bumps on my fingers cause me to scratch until my skin turns raw and my hands are burning. It has become a habit of mine to run my hands under cold water to reduce inflammation and wash the pain down the drain.

Outcome

Received national recognition from the Scholastic Art & Writing Awards with a Gold Medal award in the Mixed Media category.
