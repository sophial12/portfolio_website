Longevity Noodles

7” x 35.5” x 7”, Sculpture

A sculpture exploring the balance between life and longevity through the symbolism of noodles in Chinese culture. The piece features a bowl of exaggeratedly long noodles with small human figures climbing them, representing the journey of life from infancy to old age.

Role
Artist
Year
2023
Tools
Yarn, Clay, Resin, Bowl, Chopsticks, Wood

Project Details

The brief, approach, and results

Brief

I eat the same meal every birthday–a bowl of noodles. In Chinese culture, noodles represent longevity, so we manifest long lives by eating noodles on birthdays. In this sculpture, the length of the noodles is exaggerated to emphasize the symbolism of longevity. The noodles are shaped as a staircase, on which various figures climb the steps. From the bottom to the top, the age of the figure increases so that the figure begins their ascent as a baby and ends as an elder, symbolizing the passage of life. Furthermore, the color of the bodies blend with the noodle, but the hair progressively gets lighter to symbolize age.

Outcome

Received a Gold Key from the Scholastic Art & Writing Awards in the Sculpture category.
