About Me

Hi, my name is Sophia, and I have been using art to tell stories my entire life. My passion for creating began in childhood during a period of illness, when crayons and markers became both a distraction and a source of comfort.

As I grew older, art became a defining part of my identity. Every piece I created captured a different fragment of my story, and together, they formed a visual archive of my growth through moments of curiosity, resilience, and imagination that continue to influence how I see the world.
