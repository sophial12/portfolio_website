Down the Drain

About this piece

Eczema is a skin condition that I began suffering from seven years ago. It has traveled all around my body, but has affected my hands for the longest amount of time. The itchy, inflamed bumps on my fingers cause me to scratch until my skin turns raw and my hands are burning. It has become a habit of mine to run my hands under cold water to reduce inflammation and wash the pain down the drain.

Medium Mixed Media
Dimensions 18" x 24" x 6"
Year 2023
Award Scholastic Art and Writing Awards - Gold Medal
