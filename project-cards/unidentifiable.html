Unidentifiable

About this piece

This oil painting uses a doll gazing into their reflection with a bound and dismembered arm, symbolizing the feeling of being unable to recognize one’s self and identity as physical and mental changes take place over a lifetime. With the detached arm bound by string, the doll experiences a major change in the perception of being “whole”, while experiencing the uncanny as they stare into their distorted reflection like peeking into a portal to another world with a stranger gazing back. The billowy and flowing fabric symbolizes the flow of time and frames the reflection like a security blanket.

Medium Oil on canvas
Dimensions 16" x 20"
Year 2022
Award Scholastic Art and Writing Awards - Silver Key
