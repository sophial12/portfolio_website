Food: The Boundaries Between Raw and Ripe

About this piece

Charcuterie boards are defined as trays with a variety of sweet and savory bites. In this piece, I explored the limits to which savory and sweet foods can be paired together. This charcuterie board looks appetizing from afar, but when taking a closer look, the charcuterie board is actually composed of raw fish, lotus root, lychee, grapefruit, and tea leaves, all of which are unappealing on their own. Paying homage to the artistic practice of trompe l’oiel, the objects are painted to appear real, and the physically sculpted fish guts and found object fork add to the illusion by breaking the 2-dimensional picture plane.

Medium Acrylic on fabric
Dimensions 15" × 15" × 7"
Year 2023
